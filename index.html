The cake menu

Our cakes

Every cake is baked to order. Pick a style, choose your flavour, and we'll take care of the rest.

Birthday & celebration

Bright, fun cakes for birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers and graduations, decorated to your theme. We can do almost any flavour you can think of.

  • Vanilla
  • Chocolate
  • Red velvet
  • Marble

Wedding cakes

Elegant tiered cakes for your big day. We'll meet to plan flavours, size and design together. We offer a wide range of flavours, and we can always make small tasters to help you decide.

  • Lemon
  • Vanilla
  • Tiered

Cupcakes

Boxes of cupcakes, perfect for parties, school events, offices and gifting. Cupcakes come unfrosted unless you ask for frosting when you order.
