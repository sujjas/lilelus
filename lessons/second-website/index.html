Assignment · for Lizzie

Build your second website, with Claude

Make a new website about something you love, by telling Claude what to build, one step at a time. Claude is your helper. You decide what goes on your site.

Hand in: your website link

How to work with Claude

  1. Ask for one thing at a time. Small cakes are easier to check than giant ones.
  2. Look at what changed. Open the site and check it does what you asked.
  3. Say yes, or ask again. Not right? Tell Claude what's wrong: “Make the heading bigger and pink.”
  4. Write it down. Copy each prompt into your journal.

1. Pick your topic

Choose anything you like, except cakes (you've done those!). Some ideas:

  • My favourite book or film
  • A hobby I love
  • My dream holiday
  • An animal I find amazing
  • A recipe I can make
  • A game and how to play it

2. Build it, step by step

Each step has a prompt to try. Use your own words if you like. Then tick the boxes when you've checked it worked.

Step 1: Start the site

Try asking“Make me a new website about [my topic]. It needs a big heading, two sections of words, a list of my top 3, and a link to another website. Put it in a new GitHub repo called [name].”

Step 2: Make it look like you

Try asking“Use [colours] and a fun font. Make the buttons change colour when I hover over them. Make sure it looks good on a phone.”

Step 3: Add a photo

Try asking“Add this photo to my site, in an images folder, with alt words that describe it.” (Attach the photo.)

Step 4: Put it in the shop window

Try asking“Host my site on Vercel and connect it to my GitHub repo, so it updates by itself.”

Bonus: a button that does something

Try asking“Add a button that shows a random fun fact about [my topic] when I click it.”

3. Rules

4. Hand it in

When it's finished, send your teacher your live website link (the .vercel.app one).

Before you send it, open it on a phone and check everything works.

Stuck?

Tell Claude exactly what's wrong and what you expected. For example:

My photo doesn't show

“My photo doesn't show on the live site. Can you find out why and explain it simply?” (Phone photos saved as .HEIC often don't show: ask Claude to change it to JPG.)

My live site didn't change

“I changed my site but the .vercel.app link still shows the old one. Why?” (Often the change is still on a branch and needs merging.)

Claude did something I didn't want

“Please undo that last change.” Nothing is lost: GitHub keeps every version.
